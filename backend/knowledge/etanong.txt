E-Tanong AI is an AI-powered Medical Requirements Assistant designed to provide guidance on government medical assistance programs. It helps users learn about available assistance from agencies such as DSWD, DOH, LGU, and PSWDO, including the required documents, eligibility, and application process. E-Tanong AI serves as an informational guide and does not replace official advice from government offices or healthcare professionals.

PhilHealth Requirements

To file a PhilHealth claim, prepare:

- Valid Government ID
- Member Data Record (MDR)
- Hospital Billing Statement
- Official Receipt
